Single Sign On

Add a SAML App

How To

  1. Go to Applications → Browse App Catalog.
  2. Select the app, or create a custom SAML 2.0 integration.
  3. Enter the app's ACS URL and Entity ID.
  4. Upload Okta's metadata or certificate into the app.
  5. Assign the app to groups, not individual users.
SAML 2.0 App Catalog

SAML vs OIDC

  • SAML: XML assertions, common for enterprise web apps.
  • OIDC: JSON tokens on OAuth 2.0, common for modern and mobile apps.
  • Both remove app specific passwords and centralize access control.

Lifecycle Management

Automated Provisioning

How To

  1. Enable Provisioning on the app integration.
  2. Turn on Create, Update, and Deactivate users.
  3. Use Group Rules to assign apps by department or title.
  4. Deactivating a user in Okta removes access across connected apps.

MFA & Policies

Authentication Policy

How To

  1. Require Okta Verify push with number challenge.
  2. Prefer FastPass for phishing resistant sign in.
  3. Require stronger factors for admin and high risk apps.
  4. Deny access from untrusted network zones where appropriate.
